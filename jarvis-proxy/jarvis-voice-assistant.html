Speak commands like open Gmail, set a timer for 2 minutes, remind me in 5 minutes to stretch, or translate hello to Spanish.
Turn on Conversation Mode to talk back and forth hands-free: Jarvis listens, answers out loud, then automatically listens again.
Clap twice or whistle at any time to wake Jarvis without touching anything — the 👂 badge on the orb lights up once ambient listening is active.
Free-form questions are answered by a local proxy server (server.py) running on localhost:8787 — start it before asking anything outside the built-in commands.
